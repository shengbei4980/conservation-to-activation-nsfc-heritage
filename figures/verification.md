## Known differences

- [Figure 1](figure1/README.md): the final editable PPTX includes manual layout/material edits; its bytes are not reproduced by the archived drawing program.
- [Figure 5](figure5/README.md): an original stage label extends to 2024, while project data and submitted artwork end in 2022.
- [Figure 6](figure6/README.md): the submitted legend differs from the script, and some node sizes encode tag-hit totals rather than distinct projects. The example of 67 tag hits versus 15 distinct projects is retained. Historical time labeling also differs.
- [Figure 7](figure7/README.md): full data/program use seven categories and 142 projects; the submitted display uses six categories and 141. Both source and submission-view tables are retained.

These checks establish packaging integrity, correct source assignment and preserved computation. They do not certify the documented legend/unit/display discrepancies as resolved. Font availability and manual figure edits can affect visual reproduction.
